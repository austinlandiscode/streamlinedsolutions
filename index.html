The gap

Enterprise tech. Small business budget. That gap is closing.

Big companies run on automation, real-time data, and connected systems. Most small businesses are still running on spreadsheets, sticky notes, and software nobody's touched since 2014. That's not a discipline problem — it's an access problem. Closing it is the whole job.

Where most small businesses are

  • Booking and invoicing done by hand
  • Customer data scattered across spreadsheets
  • No follow-up unless someone remembers
  • Tools that don't talk to each other

Where Streamlined gets you

  • Booking, billing and follow-up on autopilot
  • One connected system, real-time data
  • Repetitive work handled by software
  • Systems that actually talk to each other

Process

From first call to fully running, in five steps

  1. 01

    Assess

    An on-site or virtual audit of the tools, workflows, and data you're working with today, and where the biggest gaps are.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A prioritized roadmap scoped to your actual budget, ranked by what will save the most time and money first.

  3. 03

    Implement

    We install, migrate, and configure the systems, so your team's data and history make the move with you.

  4. 04

    Train

    Hands-on sessions with your actual team, on your actual data, until everyone's confident using the new tools.

  5. 05

    Support

    Ongoing support as your business grows and your needs change, so the systems keep pace instead of falling behind again.
